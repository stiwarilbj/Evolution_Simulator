# Evolution Simulator
## Component atlas and interaction details

The desktop World opens on a white tabletop with a shallow gray wall, muted blue blobs, and olive pellets. The dark space around the tabletop is part of the composition. The player has three small marks, the clock is out at the right edge, and the first strong horizontal line is the teal time rail. Color then has a job. An olive dot is edible, a teal line moves with time, a warm red body can encode speed, and gold can mean age in a flower or energy on the floor. Shape, position, and a label distinguish these uses.

This atlas expands the [visual specification](evolution-simulator-visual-ui-spec.md) and the [website and simulation plan](evolution-simulator-website-plan.md). Use it when placing a control, drawing its parts, or deciding what the visitor sees after an action. [World detail board](evolution-simulator-world-detail.png) labels W and R components. [Settings and Trends detail board](evolution-simulator-controls-detail.png) labels S and T components. The diagrams contain original artwork and illustrative data. All pixel measurements are design coordinates at 100% zoom. The measurements and product additions below are proposed specifications.

## Palette and control paint

### Surface hierarchy and color ownership

| Surface or object | Normal paint | Small-detail rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage and chart paper | Flat **#242424** | Paint to the browser edge. Use this color between plots, under nav, and behind the board. Keep chart axes and separators local. |
| Dock and secondary controls | **#171717** | The top 84 px stats and 178 px ribbon read as one dock. Separate them with one #30302D rule. Keep the legend close to black and the flower recess at #121212. |
| Scene | White **#FFFFFF** face, source **#AAAAAA** slab material | In the opening composition, aim for rim near #CDCDCD and visible sidewall between #939393 and #AAAAAA as lit. Let the lip and wall show without drawing a black outline around the tabletop. |
| Food and bodies | Olive material **#83A51E**, steel-blue **#406780**, orange **#F47E18**, dusty pink **#CE6591** | UI swatches are brighter than the meshes. Use **#83B3CE** circle, **#F6AD73** triangle, and **#E6A0BD** diamond. Mark species on white with a 1 px #1A1A1A keyline. Keep the tiny eyes near black and the contact shadow muted. |
| Always readable text | **#FFFDFC** primary, **#EEEDD6** warm, **#BDBAB0** secondary, **#9A978C** quiet | Paint text opaquely. With #242424 behind it, quiet text is for context, not an active number. Keep headings and day labels in Latin Modern Mono. Use Source Sans Pro for explanatory copy and menu labels. |
| Actions and focus | **#33786D**, hover **#3B8176**, pressed **#2C6A60**, focus **#71D2C0** | Reserve the filled teal for Settings and one principal action per surface. Apply the pale focus ring to every control family, including dark menus and the scrubber. |
| States and caution | Info **#9DC9DD**, warning **#FADE69**, error **#E78482** | Every alert has a named state and an icon. Warning uses a triangle and error uses an outlined exclamation. Reserve red buttons for destructive confirmation. A speed trace or body gets its meaning from its key. |
| Layer overlays | Sense **#E4F5FE** multiply on white, speed **#7E635D → #C92922**, energy **#FDDE3B → #CCCCCC** | Treat floor tints as data planes and body tints as materials. Bright line colors in Trends come from the metric palette, even while Runner is active in World. |

Keep shadows black and soft. For common composites in sRGB, 65% black over #242424 gives about **#0D0D0D**, and the same scrim over white gives about **#595959**. A white 8% hover wash over #171717 gives **#2A2A2A**. A white 5% tile wash over #171717 gives **#232323**. The selected tile’s **#162E2B** rises to **#223836** under that 5% wash. These values are useful when judging whether a capture has the right contrast. Scrimmed charts retain only a silhouette of their original color. Use a separate opaque control backing where a white portion of the live scene would sit under an icon.

**Picking and gestures.** Hit-test the rendered visible bodies on release. Use the existing 5 px mouse or 10 px touch movement threshold to distinguish a selection from a camera drag. When two creatures overlap, use the closest visible body under the cursor. The **Creatures** list provides IDs and selection for small or occluded blobs.
